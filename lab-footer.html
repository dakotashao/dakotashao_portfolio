Footer · birds on wires (lab)

Move the pointer near a bird and it flies off; click empty space and they all go; they come back a few seconds later. Wires now move like heavy cable: a slow, small dip when a bird lands and a slight lift when it leaves, plus a faint sway in the wind. Version 5 adds depth: the scene shifts with the pointer, focus follows what you point at, and you can knock on the pole.

5 · Depth (new)

Wires run from a near pole back to a line of far poles. Moving the pointer shifts near and far by different amounts; focus follows it — near the pole, the pole is sharp and the distance blurs, away from it the distance comes into focus. Click the pole to knock it: the thud runs down every wire and the birds take off in the order it reaches them.
