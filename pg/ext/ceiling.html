Spec

Ceiling · one add button, items stick to the top

Concept

Press Add item and the new item is shot straight up and sticks to the ceiling. The first one sticks to the ceiling; each one after it sticks under the one before, a little off-line, the way things really stick. When five are hanging, the stack is too heavy: the next shot knocks them all down and the new item takes the ceiling.

States

Default
Pill · plus icon + label · lime #CDEB4C on #141416
Hover
The whole button collapses into one plus (70 × 70); the label slides out beside it as an outlined tag
Pressed
Plus shrinks to 95%, no bounce
Release
Plus turns 90°, the item leaves from its top arm
Disabled
Pill at 35% opacity, no hover morph, no launch

Motion

Flight
560 ms · launch decelerates, then the ceiling pulls it in (ease-in)
Stick
One frame of 5% squash, then still · items above twitch 1.5 px
Offset
Each item sticks up to ±7 px off-line and ±2° off-square, fixed once stuck
Overflow
On the 6th shot the whole stack falls, bottom first, ~1 s, gravity ease
